# Card

A surface container for grouping related content. Use `subtle`/`outlined` for content flush with
its surroundings, `elevated` when it needs to visually float above them.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Subtle} />

<Controls of={CardStories.Subtle} />

## Tokens

- `colors.surface` — subtle, outlined: background
- `colors.surfaceRaised` — elevated: background
- `colors.border` — outlined: border
- `shadow.elevated` — elevated: drop shadow
- `radii.xs` — all: corner radius
- `space.base` — all: padding

## Composition

`Card` has no layout opinion of its own — pair it with `Stack` for internal spacing.

<Canvas of={CardStories.WithButton} />

## Semantic element

Renders `<div>` by default. Override with `as="article"` when a card represents a standalone
piece of content (a post, a product, a status update) — or `section`, `header`, `footer`, `nav`,
`main`, `aside` for other structural roles. Web-only; native always renders RN's `View`.
